# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Omarchy Quick Share aims to be a welcoming, respectful project. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews or project discussions should be able to contribute without harassment, regardless of background, experience, identity, disability or nationality.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive and patient; criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experiences and communication styles. Ask clarifying questions rather than assume intent.
- Accept feedback and acknowledge mistakes. Preserve the distinction between observations, hypotheses and verified outcomes.
- Respect other people's time, consent, privacy and equipment. Do not pressure anyone to repeat approved tests or disclose credentials/private files.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, stalking, impersonation, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. This includes exposing verification codes, credentials, device identifiers, personal paths or location metadata without permission.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly. The repository owner is [PuroDelphi](https://github.com/PuroDelphi). Use a contact method the owner publicly provides to raise a concern. If no private channel is available, request a private contact route without names, private evidence or identifying allegations in a public issue. Do not publish sensitive evidence to obtain a response. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The owner may remove inappropriate content, request a correction, restrict participation or block accounts according to severity and repetition. The owner should consider context, protect reporter privacy and communicate a decision through an appropriate available channel. No guaranteed response timetable or confidential reporting mechanism is claimed here. Good-faith concerns should not result in retaliation.
